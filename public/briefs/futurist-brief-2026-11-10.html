FUTURIST TECHNOLOGY BRIEF Pacific Glazing Corporation TO: Steve Watts, Chief Executive Officer DATE: November 10, 2026 RE: Technology Signals Horizon 2029–2036

HORIZON SUMMARY

The dominant technology shift through 2036 is the emergence of protocol-driven autonomous commerce and physical-AI retrofit layers that bypass traditional OEM replacement cycles. Machine-to-machine transaction agents are not conversational LLMs but structured rule-based systems already operating in verticals like insurance claims recovery and freight arbitrage. Simultaneously, physical AI is consolidating as aftermarket software-defined tooling that retrofits existing equipment, while AI-native materials science compresses multi-physics simulation from hours to seconds, enabling real-time specification verification. Together, these forces promise to automate adversarial pricing, installation workflows, and materials validation before general procurement chatbots mature.

SIGNALS BY DOMAIN

Robotics and Automation

Trend: Physical AI and edge inference are consolidating at TRL 6–8 (evidenced by openpilot and Lucebox deployments), establishing a validated aftermarket retrofit path that upgrades existing hardware rather than forcing replacement via OEM cycles. Momentum: upward and consolidating.

Signal: Diffusion policies and structured procedural memory, first proven in surgical robotics, are beginning to transfer to trade automation. Site-specific installation footage is emerging as the scarce imitation-learning corpus for training these systems. Momentum: early but accelerating. TRL: 4–5 for trade-specific transfer.

Quantum and Computing

Trend: Open-source hardware design via gdsfactory is democratizing photonic and quantum prototyping, creating accessible fabrication pipelines for advanced optical and quantum components. Momentum: rising. TRL: 5–6.

Signal: Beneath quantum-classical hype, passive superconducting components and atomic-scale imaging are demonstrating quiet, real progress in fabrication economics. Quantum-classical hybrid systems remain froth-contaminated and uncertain. Momentum: disciplined advance in fabrication; volatility in hybrid computing. TRL: passive superconducting components at 4–5; quantum-classical hybrids at 3–4.

Energy and Materials

Trend: One-step neural operators for coupled multi-physics PDEs are compressing simulation times from hours to seconds, shifting materials verification from batch computation to real-time inference. Momentum: rapidly accelerating. TRL: 6–7.

Signal: AI-native scientific discovery stacks (including pymatgen, fipy, and deepmd-kit accessed through scientific-agent-skills libraries) are enabling agent-callable verification of thermal, structural, and molecular claims at bid time. Momentum: early adoption. TRL: 5–6.

Other Emerging — Autonomous Commerce and Adversarial Agents

Trend: Vertical adversarial agents are active now. These are not conversational LLMs but rule-based equilibrium exploiters (sudsho) operating on structured offer schemas, with operational systems already deployed in insurance-claims recovery (Rote) and freight arbitrage (LinkLane). Momentum: operational and scaling. TRL: 8–9.

Signal: Disguised agent probes are entering quote processes through human channels (email, web portals, forms) using local automation tools such as EasySpider to adversarially test pricing consistency in parallel. General procurement autonomy remains overstated for the 24-month window; realistic horizon is 36–48 months. Momentum: probing activity rising; general procurement maturing slowly. TRL: 6–7 for local automation probes; 5–6 for general procurement protocols.

CONVERGENCE WATCH

Machine-Legible Specification Commerce. Materials science stacks are merging with structured negotiation protocols. Procurement agents will soon verify U-values, wind-load ratings, and thermal claims against real-time PDE inference before committing capital, shifting bidding from relationship-based pricing to simulation-survivable specification compliance.

Aftermarket Physical AI. Edge inference and software-defined physical systems (exemplified by the openpilot retrofit model) are converging with diffusion-policy learning. Existing installation equipment will be automated through aftermarket tooling and compliant mechanisms rather than OEM-built robotic systems.

Adversarial Claims Adjudication. Insurer adjudication engines (Rote) are converging with structured installation data. The negotiation over denied restoration claims is becoming a protocol-to-protocol equilibrium contest between machine-legible counterparts, not a human negotiation.

PGC RELEVANCE TIMELINE

Near (1–3 years) — Prepare the Foundations * Insurance claims automation: Treat insurer adjudication engines (Rote) as the immediate autonomous counterparty. Build claims-negotiation capability and pricing consistency now; do not wait for general GC procurement chatbots. * Quote-process hardening: Audit internal pricing logic for arbitrage gaps. Disguised agent probes via tools like EasySpider are already entering email, portals, and forms to test pricing consistency in parallel. * Strategic data capture: Begin capturing and labeling installation footage. Site video is the scarce imitation-learning corpus for future workflow automation ownership.

Mid (3–7 years) — Capture the Retrofit and Verification Shift * Retrofit tooling deployment: Expect glazing automation to arrive as aftermarket physical-AI tooling and compliant mechanisms by roughly 2028–2030. Prepare equipment portfolios and workforce training for software-defined retrofit rather than factory robot replacement. * Simulation-survivable specifications: Thermal, structural, and molecular claims must survive real-time agent-callable verification using neural operators and scientific-agent-skills libraries at bid time.

Long (7–10 years) — Navigate Horizontal Autonomy * General machine commerce: Horizontal protocol-based procurement agents may mature (36–48 month trajectory from current state), automating broad sourcing beyond today’s vertical adversarial systems. * AI-native materials integration: Discovery pipelines fully embedded in glazing product development, potentially collapsing development timelines for new coatings, laminates, and performance substrates.

ONE WILDCARD

Passive superconducting fabrication economics mature unexpectedly for architectural glass. If the quiet progress in passive superconducting components and atomic-scale imaging translates to transparent superconducting films at commercial scale, glazing units could shift from passive thermal barriers to active energy-transport or sensing substrates. This would reclassify PGC’s product from a construction commodity into building infrastructure, opening entirely new contract structures around energy distribution and real-time environmental monitoring.
