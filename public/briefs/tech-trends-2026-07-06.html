🔗 Meta-Trends

🏗️ Physical AI Hits the Jobsite

Technologies #1, #2, #3, #7, #8, #10, #15 converge around one idea: the physical world of construction is being digitized and automated. Computer vision watches what happens, digital twins model it, robots act on it, and AI-generated 3D models feed the BIM pipeline. For PGC, the gap between "what was designed" and "what was installed" is closing fast.

🧠 The AI Stack Goes Open-Source and Self-Hosted

Technologies #4, #6, #9, #11, #12, #13 show a clear shift: the AI toolchain is commoditizing. Open-weight models (Qwen3, DeepSeek), self-hosted inference, and no-code workflow tools mean a 34-person company like PGC can build custom AI tools without enterprise budgets. The barrier to entry is now time and curiosity, not capital.

📈 From Reactive to Predictive Operations

Technologies #1, #2, #3, #5, #10, #14 all enable a shift from "fix it when it breaks" to "know it before it happens." Predictive safety monitoring, digital twin simulations, drone progress tracking, and AI-driven project finance all point toward a construction industry that plans proactively. For a glazing contractor, this means fewer callbacks, less rework, and tighter margins.

🛠️ 15 Technologies

1. Agentic AI for Construction

1
🔥 Try Now
What It Is

AI agents that plan, execute, and adapt tasks autonomously. NVIDIA's Jetson platform now supports "agentic AI" with JetPack 7.2, enabling on-site agents that monitor progress and trigger responses without human intervention.

What It's Used For

Autonomous project coordination, supply chain alerts, safety compliance checks. An agent can watch a camera feed, notice a delay in glass delivery, and automatically reschedule the install crew.

#AI-Agents#Edge-AI#Construction-Tech#Automation
MVP (3 days): Set up n8n + AI agent to monitor your project schedule. Connect to shared calendar, send daily Slack summaries of what's behind.
Why PGC: 34 people managing multiple sites. An agentic layer handles coordination overhead without adding headcount.

2. Computer Vision Safety Monitoring

2
🔥 Try Now
What It Is

AI-powered cameras that detect safety hazards in real time — missing hard hats, unauthorized zone entry, near-miss events. alwaysAI and Palcode offer construction-specific vision models running on edge devices.

What It's Used For

OSHA compliance, insurance premium reduction, real-time safety alerts, incident documentation. Systems send a text to the foreman within seconds of detecting a hazard.

#Computer-Vision#Edge-AI#Safety#Construction-Tech
MVP (5 days): Set up one AI camera at a job site entrance. Run for a week tracking hard hat compliance. Share data at weekly safety meeting.
Why PGC: Glazing involves heights, glass handling, heavy equipment. CV reduces incidents, lowers insurance costs, documents compliance automatically.

3. Digital Twins for Buildings

3
🔥 Try Now
What It Is

A continuously updated virtual replica of a physical building integrating BIM, IoT sensors, GIS data, and real-time ops. Dubai just launched a city-scale twin covering 195,000 buildings. Bentley iTwin leads in infrastructure.

What It's Used For

Pre-construction simulation, clash detection, lifecycle management. Simulate how a curtain wall performs in a 50-year storm before ordering a single panel.

#Digital-Twin#BIM#IoT#Simulation
MVP (5 days): Take one completed PGC project. Build a lightweight digital twin using Unity + IoT data. Model the glass installation sequence and compare to actual.
Why PGC: Sell "performance guarantees" instead of just glass. Show clients exactly how your facade performs thermally, structurally, aesthetically over 30 years.

4. Edge AI for Jobsite Devices

4
📊 Watch
What It Is

Running AI models on local devices instead of the cloud. New processors from NVIDIA Jetson and Qualcomm deliver 10-50 TOPS at under 5W, making on-device AI practical for construction.

What It's Used For

Real-time defect detection on glass panels as they arrive, on-site safety monitoring without internet, equipment predictive maintenance. Works on remote sites with no connectivity.

#Edge-AI#IoT#Computer-Vision#Offline
MVP (3 days): Deploy edge AI on a Raspberry Pi 5 or Jetson Nano. Connect USB camera to detect and count glass panels arriving on site. Log locally, sync when WiFi available.
Why PGC: Many PGC sites have poor connectivity. Edge AI gives real-time intelligence without depending on cellular or WiFi. No recurring cloud costs.

6. RAG + Fine-Tuning for Enterprise Knowledge

6
🔥 Try Now
What It Is

Retrieval-Augmented Generation connects LLMs to your documents; fine-tuning customizes model behavior. The 2026 toolchain (Unsloth, Ollama, Langfuse) makes both accessible to small teams. Basic RAG pipeline: $15K–$40K, 4–8 weeks.

What It's Used For

Answering questions from spec books, safety manuals, project archives. A RAG system tells a PM "we used X glazing tape on Y project and it failed — use Z instead."

#LLM#RAG#Enterprise-AI#Knowledge-Management
MVP (5 days): Take 10 PGC project close-outs + 5 safety manuals. Use Ollama + local embedding model to build a RAG chatbot. Ask it questions about past projects.
Why PGC: 34 people worth of institutional knowledge walking around. RAG captures it and makes it available to everyone — new hires, PMs, estimators.

🔥 Try Now
What It Is

What It's Used For

Custom chatbots, document analysis, code generation, workflow automation — without per-token API fees. Qwen3 Agent turns a research paper into a slide deck automatically.

#LLM#Open-Source#Self-Hosted#AI
MVP (3 days): Install Ollama on a PGC laptop. Pull Qwen3 (7B or 14B). Feed it your safety manual and ask it to generate a 5-minute glass installation safety briefing.
Why PGC: No per-token costs, no data leaving your control, full customization. For a 34-person company, open-source LLMs = AI at a fraction of enterprise pricing.

What It's Used For
