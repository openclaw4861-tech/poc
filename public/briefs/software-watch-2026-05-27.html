Fresco Try Now ⭐
Estimating / Takeoff
What it does
AI-powered automated takeoff and estimating platform purpose-built for Division 8 trades — doors, frames, and hardware. Uploads specs and drawings; Fresco extracts quantities, cross-references hardware sets, and produces a priced estimate.
Legacy alternative
Manual quantity takeoff using Bluebeam Revu or spreadsheets — flipping through hundreds of pages of specs, hand-measuring, and cross-referencing catalogs.
Key differentiator
99% accuracy claim with 70% reduction in estimator time. Purpose-built for Division 8 rather than generic construction.
Pricing
Not publicly listed. Contact sales. (YC companies typically start ~$500–$1,500/mo.)
Glazing applicability
High — Directly handles doors, frames, and hardware core to many glazing contracts.
MVP trial
Upload a submittal spec set for a curtain wall or storefront job. Test the hardware set extraction.

Speckle Try Now ⭐
CAD / BIM — Open Source
What it does
Open-source BIM interoperability platform enabling data flow between Revit, Rhino, Grasshopper, AutoCAD, Civil 3D, and more. Free when self-hosted.
Legacy alternative
BIM 360, Autodesk Construction Cloud, Trimble Connect — or emailing RVT files back and forth.
Key differentiator
Only open-source option in BIM interoperability. Unlimited free self-hosted access. Extensive native integrations across AECO toolchain.
Pricing
Free self-hosted; cloud from ~$8/user/mo. No commercial license needed.
Glazing applicability
High — Enables PGC to receive BIM models from GCs (Revit) and push to fabrication equipment without format-conversion pain.
MVP trial
Self-host and upload a current project IFC file. Test sending Rhino-generated detail to a GC's Revit file.

Zoo (zoo.dev) Watch 👁
CAD / BIM — AI Text-to-CAD
What it does
One of the first publicly available text-to-CAD platforms. Type a prompt ("a flange with 6 bolt holes") and it generates 3D geometry using ML. Agentic reasoning + self-correction during generation. Built on open-source modeling engine.
Legacy alternative
AutoCAD, Fusion 360, SolidWorks — starting from a blank canvas every time.
Key differentiator
First-mover in text-to-CAD with agentic reasoning. Can inspect, snapshot, and debug geometry during generation. Open-source engine underneath.
Pricing
Freemium model. Specific tiers not publicly listed.
Glazing applicability
Medium — Similar to AdamCAD; useful for prototyping shop fixtures and fabrication tools. Not production-critical yet.
MVP trial
Access zoo.dev/design-studio. Prompt: "storefront glass retainer clip with mounting tab."
