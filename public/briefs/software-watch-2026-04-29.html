This Week's Software Landscape

The construction software stack is finally waking up from its 20-year slumber. This week's scan found 10 new or recently launched tools targeting the gaps left by AutoCAD, Revit, Procore, and similar legacy platforms. The pattern is clear: cloud-native, AI-powered, cheaper, and built for how people actually work. Three categories dominate: (1) AI-powered plan review, (2) Cloud CAD/BIM, and (3) Specialized field tools.
