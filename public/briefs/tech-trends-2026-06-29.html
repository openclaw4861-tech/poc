🔍 Meta-Trends

Meta-Trend 1: AI Is Eating Construction — Finally

After years of AI transforming software, media, and finance, construction tech is having its moment. Five of the 15 technologies on this list are directly applicable to construction workflows: computer vision takeoffs, AI photo updates, OpenConstructionERP, Firmus design review, and AI scheduling. The common thread: AI is moving from "interesting demo" to "saves real money" in construction. The window for early adopter advantage is now — tools that automate estimating, scheduling, and client communication are mature enough to deploy today.

Meta-Trend 2: The Cost of AI Is Crashing

Three trends point in the same direction: AI is getting dramatically cheaper. OpenAI's custom chip (Jalapeño) will reduce inference costs. Asian foundation models create price competition. Decentralized training (BlockTrain) could democratize model development. Meanwhile, open-source alternatives (OpenConstructionERP, open-design) eliminate software licensing costs entirely. The "wait for prices to come down" argument is losing force — the smart play is to start experimenting now with free/open-source tools, so when prices drop further, PGC already knows which tools work.

Meta-Trend 3: Voice and Video Are the New Interfaces

Wan-Streamer (real-time video AI), AI voice agents, and Flow (voice-first platform) all point to a future where we interact with software through conversation, not clicks. The keyboard-and-mouse interface is being supplemented — and in some cases replaced — by natural speech and real-time video AI. Construction is a hands-on, voice-first industry. Field crews don't want to type. Customers want to talk to someone (or something) that understands them. Voice and video AI interfaces are a natural fit for PGC's workflow.

1. Wan-Streamer — Real-time Interactive Foundation Models AI / Video
What it is
Alibaba's Wan-Streamer is a native-streaming, end-to-end interactive foundation model that handles language, audio, and video as both input and output in a single Transformer. Achieves ~200ms model-side latency and ~550ms total interaction latency — meaning it can hold a real-time video conversation with you, complete with facial expressions and voice. Released June 23, 2026 on arXiv (2606.25041).
