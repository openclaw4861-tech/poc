1 OpenConstructionERP v3.0 🟢 High PGC relevance Try Now

What it does: Open-source construction ERP with professional BOQ (Bill of Quantities), 4D scheduling, 5D cost model, AI-powered estimation, and CAD/BIM/PDF takeoff — all in one platform. 71 modules, 42 regional catalogs, 21 languages. AGPL-3.0 open source. Installed via pip install openconstructionerp.

Legacy alternative: Modern replacement for Sage 300, CMiC, or custom spreadsheet-based estimating. Also competes with Autodesk Costing and Procore's estimating module.

Key differentiator: True open-source — no per-seat licensing, no vendor lock-in. PDF and CAD takeoff means it can extract glazing quantities directly from Division 08 specs and drawings. AI cost matching applies historical pricing to a new BOQ automatically.

Pricing: Free (AGPL-3.0). Self-hosted. Commercial use requires AGPL license (or you can purchase a commercial license).

3 Buildots — AI Site Progress Monitoring 🟡 Medium PGC relevance Watch

What it does: Cloud-based construction progress tracking using helmet-mounted 360° cameras and computer vision. Workers wear cameras during regular site walks; AI compares footage against the BIM model and project schedule to automatically flag deviations across 80+ construction stages. 2026 updates added better subcontractor performance scoring and drone integration for aerial progress capture.

Legacy alternative: Manual site walks with photos, written progress reports, and monthly BIM-vs-field comparisons. Replaces the "surprise discovery" pattern where problems are found weeks after they've become expensive.

Key differentiator: Buildots customers report 25% faster completion times through automated deviation detection. AI-generated mitigation suggestions help superintendents prioritize what's most likely to cause delays. Works across the full construction lifecycle.

Pricing: Enterprise pricing only (no public pricing). Targets large GCs and developers with complex projects.

4 Workyard — GPS Time Tracking + Job Costing 🟢 High PGC relevance Try

What it does: GPS-verified time clock and job costing for field crews. Founded by a former contractor who ran a 700-person business — it shows. Geofenced clock-ins prevent "truck clocking" (workers clocking in from home before arriving at the site). Automated cost code assignment, works offline on low-signal sites, direct QuickBooks integration. Time entries connect to job and cost codes with location data embedded — no separate add-on needed.

Legacy alternative: Paper timesheets, Excel labor tracking, or expensive multi-module ERP systems for time tracking. Replaces "punch cards + spreadsheets" that most small-to-mid glazing contractors still use.

Key differentiator: Built for crews who don't want to learn complex software — 5-minute onboarding. Accurate labor cost per job is the key output: know exactly what each project cost in man-hours, not just total payroll. Founder experience shows in the field-first UX.

Pricing: Per-user, per month (not publicly listed — estimated $8-15/user/mo based on industry comparisons). Free trial available.

🧪 MVP trial: Have one PGC crew use Workyard for one week on a project. Compare GPS-verified hours against manual time cards from the same week. Calculate how much "truck clocking" or rounding was costing you. workyard.com | 1 week trial
