Rudus

Estimating / Takeoff

What it does: AI-powered takeoff and estimation platform purpose-built for concrete contractors. Automatically identifies footings, walls, columns, and slabs on plans. Claims 70% reduction in estimation time and 3x more projects won. YC S26.

Legacy alt: On-Screen Takeoff + manual pricing

Differentiator: Niche focus on concrete means the AI is trained specifically for concrete plan symbols and assemblies.

Pricing: Not public (YC S26)

Glazing: Medium

MVP: Not directly applicable, but validates the niche-trade AI estimation thesis.

Helonic

CAD/BIM

What it does: Analyzes PDF construction plans to automatically detect clashes and inconsistencies across architectural, structural, and MEP drawings. Drafts RFIs so teams resolve issues before ground breaks. Integrates with Procore and Autodesk. YC S26.

Legacy alt: Manual BIM coordination meetings + Navisworks

Differentiator: Works from PDFs (no native BIM model required), catches cross-discipline conflicts in preconstruction, auto-drafts RFIs.

Pricing: Not public (YC S26)

Glazing: Medium

MVP: Curtain wall and glazing systems often clash with structural/MEP — catching those in preconstruction saves significant rework.

Try Now

Legacy alt: QuickBooks + manual paper processing

Differentiator: Purpose-built for construction back-office workflows, not generic accounting. AI agents handle document processing.

Pricing: Not public (YC S26)

Glazing: High

Analytics / Estimating

Legacy alt: RSMeans + manual benchmarking

Resolve

CAD/BIM / AR-VR

What it does: Enables construction teams to detect design issues in 3D BIM as early as possible. Combines real-time collaboration, VR-based BIM reviews, and AI to identify risks earlier and coordinate faster across mission-critical infrastructure. YC-backed.

Legacy alt: Navisworks + in-person coordination meetings

Differentiator: VR-native BIM review with AI risk detection. Built for infrastructure-scale projects.

Pricing: Not public (YC-backed)

Glazing: Medium

MVP: VR review of curtain wall BIM models could catch installation sequencing issues before they hit the field.

Watch

Scope3D

BIM / Digital Twins

What it does: Converts BIM and construction files into fast, web-native 3D twins. Owners, builders, architects, and contractors use them to scope, price, renovate, furnish, maintain, and forecast CapEx. YC-backed (formerly WeReno).

Legacy alt: Matterport scans + manual BIM export

Differentiator: Web-native (no software install), works from existing BIM files, built for the full building lifecycle.

Pricing: Not public (YC-backed)

Glazing: High

MVP: 3D building twins are ideal for retrofit glazing projects — walk a building digitally, scope the glass replacement, measure openings.

ERP (Open-Source)

What it does: Open-source construction ERP with BOQ, PDF/CAD/BIM takeoff, AI cost matching. 42 regional catalogues, 21 languages, 71 modules. v3.0 just released. AGPL-3.0 licensed.

Legacy alt: Sage 100 Contractor / Acumatica

Differentiator: Free, open-source, self-hosted. 71 modules cover everything from estimating to project management to accounting. AI cost matching across 42 regional catalogues.

Pricing: Free (AGPL-3.0) — pip install openconstructionerp

Glazing: High

MVP: pip install openconstructionerp and spin up a test instance. No per-seat licensing costs.
