FlowManual

Bidding / Procurement

What it does: All-in-one AI bidding and purchasing platform for general and specialty contractors. Automates the buying process during pre-construction — from plan upload to supplier quotes. Built by two engineers and Harvard dropouts. YC S26.

Legacy alt: Manual spreadsheet bidding + phone/email supplier sourcing

Differentiator: End-to-end from bid to purchase order in one platform. Built for both GCs and specialty trades.

Pricing: Not public (YC S26, seed-stage)

Glazing: High

MVP: Upload glass shop drawings, get supplier-matched quotes and POs.

Legacy alt: Manual takeoff (Bluebeam + Excel) + Word proposals

Differentiator: Full pipeline from plan → takeoff → estimate → proposal in one AI-native workflow. No CAD plugin needed.

Pricing: Not public (YC S26, seed-stage)

Glazing: High

MVP: Upload glass shop drawings, get structured takeoffs and proposals.

Helonic

CAD / BIM

What it does: Analyzes PDF construction plans to automatically detect clashes and inconsistencies across architectural, structural, and MEP drawings. Drafts RFIs automatically. Integrates with Procore and Autodesk. YC S26.

Legacy alt: Manual cross-referencing of drawing sets + manual RFI drafting

Differentiator: Catches coordination errors in preconstruction (not mid-build), preventing costs that routinely reach hundreds of thousands per project.

Pricing: Not public (YC S26, seed-stage)

Glazing: High

MVP: Upload PGC glass shop drawings against structural/MEP plans to catch clashes before fabrication.

Try Now

OpenConstructionERP

ERP / Inventory

What it does: Open-source construction ERP with 71 modules including BOQ, PDF/CAD/BIM takeoff, AI cost matching. 42 regional catalogues, 21 languages. AGPL-3.0. v3.0.

Legacy alt: Sage, Viewpoint, or other commercial construction ERPs ($10k+/year)

Differentiator: Free, open-source, self-hosted. Massive module library. AI cost matching across 42 regional catalogues.

Pricing: Free (AGPL-3.0, self-hosted)

Glazing: High

MVP: pip install openconstructionerp — immediate self-hosted trial with inventory and BOQ modules.
