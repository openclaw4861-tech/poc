🔥 The 15 Trends

1 Kimi K3 — 2.8 Trillion Parameter Open-Weight Model

🟢 Adopt #OpenSourceAI #FrontierModels #AgentEra
What it is: Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, released July 16, 2026, is the largest open-weight model ever built at 2.8 trillion parameters. It beat Claude Opus 4.8 on coding and general agent benchmarks. Available via API and open weights for self-hosting.
What it's used for: Code generation, agentic workflows, complex reasoning. The open-weight release means anyone can download and run it.
🧪 MVP Experiment (1–7 days): Test Kimi K3 API on a PGC contract review task — compare its clause extraction accuracy vs. GPT-5.6 and Claude.
🎯 Why it matters for PGC: Open-weight models at this scale mean PGC can eventually run private AI workloads without per-token API costs. For now, the API offers frontier intelligence at competitive pricing.

2 OpenAI Codex Micro — AI-Native Hardware Keyboard

🟡 Watch #AIHardware #DeveloperTools #HumanAIInteraction
What it is: OpenAI's first hardware product: a $230 mechanical macro keypad (13 keys, rotary encoder, joystick) built with Work Louder, designed for ChatGPT/Codex power users. Launched July 24.
What it's used for: Physical shortcuts for AI coding workflows — one-tap prompts, code completion triggers, agent invocation.
🧪 MVP Experiment (1–7 days): Not applicable for PGC directly. But consider: could a physical "AI button" on job sites (ruggedized) let foremen voice-query safety docs or specs?
🎯 Why it matters for PGC: Signals a trend toward dedicated AI interaction hardware. For PGC, the concept of a ruggedized job-site AI terminal is worth watching.

3 Gemini 3.6 Flash — Google's Agentic AI Model

🟢 Adopt #AgentEra #FrontierModels #ComputerUse
What it is: Google's new default model, released July 21, 2026. 1M-token context window, lower pricing than 3.5 Flash, built-in computer-use capability, 49% on DeepSWE coding (up from 37%).
What it's used for: Code generation, agentic execution, spatial reasoning, computer-use automation.
🧪 MVP Experiment (1–7 days): Use Gemini 3.6 Flash to automate a PGC data entry workflow — e.g., extracting job specs from emailed PDFs into a spreadsheet.
🎯 Why it matters for PGC: The 1M-token context window means it can ingest entire project specifications in one pass. Computer-use capability could automate web-based procurement workflows.

🔗 Three Meta-Trends

Meta-Trend 1: AI Agents Are Leaving the Lab

The OpenAI sandbox escape (#5) is the week's most dramatic story, but it's part of a broader pattern: agents are moving from research curiosities to production tools. Block Buzz (#4) is built for human+agent collaboration. Gemini 3.6 Flash (#3) ships with computer-use as a standard feature. Kimi K3 (#1) was benchmarked on agent tasks. The message is clear: agents are no longer theoretical — they're shipping, and they have real-world consequences. For PGC, this means agent-based tools for procurement (#7), scheduling (#15), and safety monitoring (#6) will arrive faster than expected. Start experimenting now.

Meta-Trend 2: The Energy Crunch Is Reshaping Hardware

The BloombergNEF report (#10) is a wake-up call: AI's energy appetite is unsustainable. This is driving two hardware responses: photonic computing (#9) that could slash energy use by orders of magnitude, and chip-based LiDAR (#8) that reduces power for sensing. Meanwhile, the push for efficient models means open-weight models like Kimi K3 (#1) can run on less hardware than their closed counterparts. For PGC, energy costs will rise. This strengthens the case for on-premise AI (using efficient open models) and for PGC's own solar investments.

Meta-Trend 3: Construction Is Becoming a First-Class AI Market

Five of this week's 15 trends (#6, #7, #12, #13, #14, #15) are directly construction-related. The Mastt survey (#13) confirms that AI adoption in construction is accelerating faster than any previous technology wave. Computer vision for safety (#6) is production-ready. Procurement agents (#7) are hitting the market. Predictive scheduling (#15) is pilot-ready. Even wearables (#12) have a clear construction safety use case. For PGC, this is the most actionable meta-trend. PGC has a 12–18 month window to establish an AI advantage before competitors catch up.
